      How these numbers work

      Statistics cover jobs still on the selected board, across all time, regardless of the board search box or XP mode. Deleted jobs are excluded. Moving a job back and forth never adds another job to a count.

      Stage visits come from the job timeline, recorded XP events, and its current column. A conversion requires evidence that the destination came after the starting stage. Skipped stages are not filled in. Custom columns are never automatically classified as a response, interview, offer, or rejection.

      Older jobs can have incomplete history. They still count in their known stages, but an undated or ambiguous visit may not establish a conversion. Weekly activity uses the first dated Applied record, with the entered application date as a fallback only for jobs known to have reached Applied. Response time uses dated stage records.
